# Recommendation Rules

Rules translate account facts into practical technical consulting moves.

## Controls Modernization Discovery

Triggered when older or limited-support PLC platforms combine with high downtime, high support risk, or obsolete lifecycle status.

Evidence to collect:

- Controller revision list
- Critical line ranking
- Spares availability
- Downtime by asset family

## Drive Health And Predictive Maintenance

Triggered when a high motor or drive count combines with downtime pain involving drives, motors, conveyors, pumps, crushers, presses, or web handling.

Evidence to collect:

- Drive fault history
- Motor load profiles
- Critical spares list
- Maintenance work orders

## HMI/SCADA Standardization

Triggered when a multi-line operation has poor visibility, manual reporting, alarm overload, or inconsistent operator interfaces.

Evidence to collect:

- HMI inventory
- Alarm counts
- Shift report examples
- Line performance metrics

## Safety Lifecycle Review

Triggered by safety system age of 12 or more years, safety reset issues, aging safety devices, or safety lifecycle notes.

Evidence to collect:

- Safety device inventory
- Validation records
- Bypass logs
- Risk assessment date

## Digital Simulation Workshop

Triggered by process variability, recipe complexity, scale-up pressure, batch variation, yield variability, or major capacity planning.

Evidence to collect:

- Process parameters
- Current constraints
- Changeover recipes
- Planned capacity changes

## Short-Cycle Service Opportunity

Triggered by existing service activity, active support context, or recent project work that can shorten discovery.

Evidence to collect:

- Open service items
- Recent callout notes
- Stakeholder list
- Known outage windows

## Analytics And Edge Monitoring

Triggered by usable or rich data availability, meaningful sensor footprint, and a practical performance or downtime use case.

Evidence to collect:

- Tag list
- Data retention rules
- Event codes
- Network access constraints

## Discovery-First Positioning

Triggered when a competitor or incumbent supplier is already present.

Evidence to collect:

- Current supplier scope
- Decision criteria
- Pain points not covered today
- Contract timing

## Assessment Workshop

Triggered when installed-base confidence or data availability is low.

Evidence to collect:

- Asset inventory
- Network map
- Downtime examples
- Decision owner
