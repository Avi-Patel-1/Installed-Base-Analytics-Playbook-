# Example Playbook

## Account Snapshot

Apex Packaging Systems is a high-speed packaging site with 9 production lines, mature controls, a large drive and sensor footprint, and active modernization interest.

## Why This Matters

The account has measurable downtime exposure, support risk, and a near-term format launch. Those conditions make the next conversation more concrete than a broad technology review.

## What To Ask

- Which line is most affected by motion faults or alarm overload?
- How are format launch risks currently being measured?
- What outage window is available before commissioning?
- Which stakeholder owns downtime cost validation?
- What evidence would make a modernization discovery credible internally?

## What To Propose

Start with a controls modernization discovery. Review PLC/HMI standards, alarm priorities, drive fault history, safety lifecycle status, and launch constraints. Convert the findings into a phased scope with one near-term reliability action and one longer-term platform decision.

## What Evidence To Collect

- Controller revision list
- Alarm counts by line
- Drive fault history
- Last safety validation record
- Downtime examples tied to format changeover

## What Success Could Look Like

The first milestone is a short, evidence-backed roadmap that helps the customer reduce commissioning risk, stabilize line performance, and justify the next funded scope.
