# Playbook Format

The playbook generator creates an editable customer engagement document for the selected account.

## Sections

- Executive Summary
- Account Snapshot
- Likely Pain Points
- Installed-Base Assumptions
- Evidence Behind Recommendation
- Discovery Questions
- Recommended Workshop Agenda
- Proposed Solution Architecture
- Value Hypothesis
- Data Needed From Customer
- Potential Objections
- Risk Mitigation Points
- Follow-Up Actions
- Proposal Outline

## Customer-Facing Language

The generated text is intended to be practical and direct:

- Why this matters
- What to ask
- What to propose
- What evidence to collect
- What success could look like

## Export Options

The app can export the selected account playbook as Markdown or HTML. The on-screen playbook is editable before export. Discovery questions can also be copied to the clipboard.
