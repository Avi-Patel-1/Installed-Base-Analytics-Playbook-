# Methodology

The project models a technical consulting workflow for industrial automation accounts. The core question is not simply which customer is largest; it is which account has enough installed-base risk, operational pain, account readiness, and data evidence to justify a credible next conversation.

## Workflow

1. Start with installed-base facts: controls platform, HMI/SCADA, drives, sensors, safety age, lifecycle stage, support risk, service history, data availability, and current pain points.
2. Score each account across lifecycle risk, complexity, modernization fit, service urgency, data readiness, and sales readiness.
3. Segment the account into a practical pursuit category.
4. Map triggered facts to recommendation rules.
5. Generate an account playbook with questions, evidence needs, workshop agenda, value hypothesis, risks, and follow-up actions.
6. Export ranking, opportunity summary, scoring explanation, and customer-facing playbook outputs.

## Interpreting The Dashboard

- High technical consulting priority means the account has a strong combined signal across risk, fit, urgency, readiness, and opportunity value.
- High lifecycle risk alone does not guarantee a proposal-ready account. Low data readiness or weak buying trigger may require an assessment workshop first.
- High data readiness means a monitoring or analytics pilot can be scoped faster because instrumentation and confidence are already stronger.
- Competitive presence changes positioning. It does not remove the opportunity, but it shifts the first move toward discovery and evidence.

## Segment Use

Segments are designed to guide the next move:

- Quick win: near-term customer conversation.
- Modernization candidate: installed-base assessment and phased upgrade path.
- Strategic enterprise account: roadmap discussion across larger operations.
- Service-risk account: lifecycle and supportability review.
- Data-readiness candidate: focused monitoring or analytics pilot.
- Education/nurture account: evidence gathering before proposal.
- Low priority: monitor for triggers.
